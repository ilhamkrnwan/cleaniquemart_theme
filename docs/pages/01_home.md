## 4. Implementasi Tema WordPress (`cleaniquemart-theme`)

- **File Template**: `front-page.php`
- **Template Parts yang Dipanggil**:
  ```php
  get_template_part('template-parts/hero-home');
  get_template_part('template-parts/metrics-bar');
  get_template_part('template-parts/problem-solution');
  get_template_part('template-parts/market-segments');
  get_template_part('template-parts/variants-grid');
  get_template_part('template-parts/testimonials-real');
  get_template_part('template-parts/reviews-lightbox');
  get_template_part('template-parts/packages-table');
  get_template_part('template-parts/steps-join');
  get_template_part('template-parts/cta-banner');
  ```
- **Customizer / ACF Fields yang Dibutuhkan**:
  - `home_hero_title`: Teks judul utama
  - `home_hero_badge`: Teks badge atas
  - `home_wa_phone`: Nomor WhatsApp CS (`087885590088`)
  - `home_proposal_url`: URL Google Drive Proposal
